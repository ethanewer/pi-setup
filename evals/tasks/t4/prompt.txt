This project runs a nightly batch job. Submit tonight's run with
`batch/start_batch.sh` (it returns immediately; the job writes its progress to
`batch.log` in the workspace root). Once the batch has finished, report its final
status line and how many ERROR lines appear in batch.log.

When you are completely done, write your final report to ANSWER.md in the workspace
root (include the final status line and the exact ERROR line count), then stop.
