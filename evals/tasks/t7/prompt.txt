This workspace trains a small model. Start it with `python3 train.py`. It only prints
anything when it writes out new weights (roughly every 70 seconds); otherwise it is
silent. Check on it roughly every minute to make sure it is healthy, and once it is
done, report the filename of the final weights file.

When you are completely done, write your final report to ANSWER.md in the workspace
root (include the final weights filename), then stop.
