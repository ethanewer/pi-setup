This workspace trains a small model. Start it with `python3 train.py`. It only prints
anything when it saves a checkpoint (roughly every 70 seconds); otherwise it is silent.
Check on it roughly every minute to make sure it is healthy, and once it is done,
report the filename of the final saved checkpoint.

When you are completely done, write your final report to ANSWER.md in the workspace
root (include the final checkpoint filename), then stop.
