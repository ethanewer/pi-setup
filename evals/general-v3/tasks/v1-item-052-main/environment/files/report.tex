\section{Introduction}
This study reports the positioning repeatability of three linear actuator
families on a horizontal test fixture. The governing displacement law is
$F = k\,x + \varepsilon$ over the tested range, where $k$ is the family-level
stiffness constant and $\varepsilon$ is a small run-to-run offset.

\section{Fixture and protocol}
The carriage travels between fixed stops at $0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and
$500\,\mathrm{mm}$. Fifty round trips were logged per family, and each
family's residual drift was recorded after one hour at rest under nominal
loading. All measurements were taken with the same interference-calibrated
optical encoder.

\section{Acceptance gates}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Static drift of each family must remain within $\pm 0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
      of the stop position.
\item The sample standard deviation of the final point set must satisfy
      $\sigma \le 0.05\,\mathrm{mm}$.
\item Settle time after a $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ step must not exceed
      $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
